# Thumbnail Taste

Thumbnail Taste Deck (v3). Right = Yes, left = No, up or Love button = Love, down or Maybe button = Maybe, Skip records nothing. Optional note per vote. Storage key thumbswipe_v3 (migrates v2 and v1; I becomes M). Submit To Scout posts to SUBMIT_URL in index.html (null: no team pipeline found Oct 13), otherwise saves locally and downloads JSON.
Progress is saved in your browser. At the end, tap **Copy Results** (compact JSON: `[card id, L|Y|N]`) and paste it to Scout, or Download JSON.

`manifest.json` lists every card with attributes (source, text_words, accent_color, text_treatment, graphic, subject, grading, position, sticker, and so on).
Real-Short thumbnails are from YouTube (i.ytimg.com) for private taste research only.
